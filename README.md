# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee data and transform raw HR information into meaningful, actionable business insights.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of the organization's workforce by analyzing key HR metrics such as **employee headcount, attrition, demographics, salary distribution, job roles, job satisfaction, performance, and employee experience**.

The primary objective of this project is to help HR teams and business stakeholders **identify workforce trends, understand employee attrition patterns, evaluate employee satisfaction, and make data-driven decisions for better workforce management and retention**.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall employee workforce distribution.
* Monitor and understand **employee attrition** and attrition rate.
* Identify employee groups with higher attrition levels.
* Analyze workforce distribution across **departments and job roles**.
* Understand the relationship between **salary, experience, and employee attrition**.
* Analyze employee satisfaction and work-life balance.
* Study employee demographics such as **age and gender**.
* Identify patterns that can help organizations improve **employee retention and workforce planning**.
* Provide an interactive dashboard for quick and effective HR decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard includes multiple analytical views covering:

### 👥 Workforce Overview

Provides a high-level summary of the organization's workforce using KPI cards and visualizations, including employee count and other important HR metrics.

### 📉 Attrition Analysis

Analyzes employee attrition patterns and helps identify potential factors associated with employee turnover.

Attrition can be explored across different dimensions such as:

* Age group
* Gender
* Department
* Job role
* Salary
* Experience
* Job satisfaction
* Work-life balance

### 🧑‍💼 Employee Demographics

Provides insights into the workforce based on demographic characteristics such as:

* Age
* Gender
* Employee distribution
* Other available employee attributes

### 💼 Job & Department Analysis

Analyzes employee distribution across different departments and job roles, helping understand workforce composition and areas with higher employee turnover.

### 💰 Salary Analysis

Examines salary distribution and its relationship with employee attrition and other workforce characteristics.

### ⭐ Satisfaction & Engagement

Analyzes employee satisfaction and engagement indicators to understand how workplace experience may influence employee retention.

### 📊 Performance & Experience

Explores employee performance and years of experience to identify patterns in workforce behavior and attrition.

## 🔄 Data Preparation & Transformation

The HR dataset was prepared and transformed using **Power Query** before creating the dashboard.

The data preparation process included:

* Removing unnecessary records
* Handling missing/null values
* Removing duplicate records
* Correcting inconsistent data
* Transforming columns into appropriate data types
* Creating calculated columns where required
* Preparing the dataset for analysis and visualization

## 🧮 DAX & Data Modeling

DAX measures were created to calculate important HR metrics and KPIs, including metrics such as **employee count and attrition rate**.

These calculations enable the dashboard to dynamically respond to user selections and provide interactive analysis across different employee segments.

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive **slicers and filters** that allow users to drill down into specific employee segments and analyze HR metrics from different perspectives.

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard based on relevant HR attributes and quickly identify patterns and trends within the workforce.

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard can help HR and management teams answer questions such as:

* How many employees are currently part of the organization?
* What is the overall employee attrition rate?
* Which departments or job roles have higher attrition?
* Which age groups are more likely to leave?
* Does salary have an impact on employee retention?
* How does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
* How does employee experience influence retention?
* Which employee segments require greater attention from HR?
* What workforce trends can support better retention strategies?
